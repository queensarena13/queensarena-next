# QueensArena Instagram Growth Plan

Account: https://www.instagram.com/queensarena.app/

## Goal

Build trust and early audience before paid data/marketing. The Instagram should show that QueensArena is useful, focused on women athletes, and alive every week.

## Weekly Rhythm

- Monday: weekly fixtures carousel.
- Tuesday: team or player spotlight.
- Wednesday: standings/ranking snapshot.
- Thursday: throwback or historical data since 2020.
- Friday: weekend watchlist.
- Matchday: story with live score/update when available.
- Sunday: results recap and feedback question.

## Rules

- Use Portuguese from Portugal first; add short English line only when useful.
- Keep captions short and confident.
- Tag clubs, leagues and players only when the post is factual and respectful.
- Do not use logos/images unless we have permission or they are from official shareable sources.
- Always include one action: save, share, comment, beta link or follow.
